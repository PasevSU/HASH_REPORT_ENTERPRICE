ChronoSeal v5.3.6 RC - OpenSSL RFC3161 ByteRange verifier correction

Observed on Windows/OpenSSL 4.0.2:
  openssl ts -verify -token_in -in signed.bin -CAfile ca.pem
  -> ts: Use -help for summary.

Correct invocation:
  openssl ts -verify -token_in -in token.tsr -data signed.bin -CAfile ca.pem

The token and exact PDF ByteRange-selected data are written separately. Final-reopen verification reuses the same independent verifier. No GUI, report layout, SINGLE/COMBO, recursion or TSA topology changes.
