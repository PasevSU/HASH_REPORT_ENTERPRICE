2.1 test_web_workspace_reads_the_same_tokens - REAL REGRESSION
    The v5.11.0 version bump updated chronoseal/branding.py but ui/index.html
    still carried "v5.10.0-gui" in two places. The suite correctly proved that
    the web workspace and the desktop app disagreed on the version.
    A follow-up sweep then found a third missed spot: the header comment of
    ui/style.css. Total corrected strings: ui/index.html x2, ui/style.css x1.
    Verified: "v5.11.0-gui" in html = True, residual "5.10" in ui/ = 0
    (3 files scanned).

2.2 test_t27_heartbeat_output_keeps_a_slow_child_alive - ENVIRONMENTAL FLAKE
    idle_timeout was 0.9 s against a 0.2 s heartbeat. Pipeline._run starts its
    idle clock when Popen returns, so Python's cold start on the SMB share was
    charged against the budget and the watchdog fired before the child had
    printed a single line ("The child produced no output at all before
    stalling."). Product behaviour was correct; only the test budget was wrong
    for this share. The product code was deliberately NOT weakened.
    Fix: HEARTBEAT_IDLE_TIMEOUT = 2.5 with a 0.5 s heartbeat (12 iterations =
    6 s total). A silent child is still killed long before it finishes, which
    is exactly what the test exists to prove. Applied to t27 and to its latent
    twin t29.
    test_t28 keeps idle_timeout=0.9 on purpose - it must die fast.
